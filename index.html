Chords

Chord plays

The bottom three rows play the chord on each scale degree, voiced three ways, so everything stays in the key. The ten blocks above change that chord while you hold them: the key's own 7th, dominant, major and minor sevenths, sixths, sus4, diminished and more. With a mouse, click a block to switch it on and off.

–Play the right side of the LinnStrument, or these keys.

Notes from the right zone pass straight through, including slide and pressure. A dot marks the notes of the key.

First-time setup

  1. On the LinnStrument, turn Split on. The defaults work: left side on MIDI channels 1–8, right side on 9–16.
  2. In Global Settings, set Row Offset to No Overlap so every pad on the left has its own note.
  3. Click Connect MIDI and pick your LinnStrument. Then press Learn and tap three pads when asked.
